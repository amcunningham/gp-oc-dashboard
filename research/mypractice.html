These are associations, not guarantees: practices that improved a measure were not always rewarded, and your circumstances may differ. Survey estimates for small practices are noisy (flagged where the sample is under 50 responses). "Calls ended within the IVR" blends automated service, redirection to other channels, and callers giving up in the menus — published data cannot distinguish them. Phone measures cover only practices whose telephony supplier submits to the national collection (about 85%). Appointment counts reflect what is recorded in appointment books, not total workload. Sources: Cloud Based Telephony (Mar–May 2026), Online Consultation Submissions, Appointments in General Practice, GP Patient Survey 2012–2026. Evidence for each claim: PANEL_NOTES.md.
